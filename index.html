Tip

Look at loot and press F to pick it up. Cabinets, fridges, lockers and car trunks can all be searched.

Gunshots carry for hundreds of metres. A suppressor, or a machete, keeps the dead from following you home.

Water first, then food. Rain barrels, taps and coconuts will keep you alive; seawater will not.

Bleeding kills fast. Rip a shirt into rags with Tab and bandage up before you do anything else.

Police stations, gun stores and the military bases hold the best weapons, and the most infected.

Boats and the tour helicopters are the only way between the islands. Keep a jerrycan of fuel.

Press T for chat and type /help for commands like /give, /tp, /time, /locate and /summon.

The summits of Mauna Kea and Mauna Loa are freezing. Dress warm before you climb.
